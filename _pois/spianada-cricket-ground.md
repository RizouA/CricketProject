## English
The **Spianada Cricket Ground** is located in the heart of Corfu’s Spianada Square  
and is the oldest and most emblematic cricket field in Greece.  
Its location bridges the city’s sporting heritage with its cultural identity,  
hosting matches, exhibitions, and annual events.
